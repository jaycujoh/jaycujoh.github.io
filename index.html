ZION HUD

A private home dashboard for one person.

ZION HUD is a personal dashboard that runs on a small home server. It shows the owner's own home-lab status, messages and calendar in one place. It is not a public service: there are no sign-ups and no other users.

Google Calendar

The owner connects their own Google account so the dashboard can show their calendar and let them create, edit, move and delete their events from the dashboard. Changes made in the dashboard go straight to Google Calendar, and changes made elsewhere appear in the dashboard.

Privacy

Calendar data stays on the owner's home server and is never shared or sold. Read the privacy policy.
